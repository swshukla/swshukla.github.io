VideoForge

VideoForge is a private, single-user tool that publishes the Null Pointer channel's own explainer videos. It runs on its operator's own computer and acts only on the operator's own accounts.

What it does

  • Uploads the channel's long videos and Shorts to YouTube, with their titles, descriptions, thumbnails, captions and playlists, after the operator has watched and approved each one.
  • Posts the approved Shorts to the channel's Instagram account as Reels.
  • Posts short insights from each video, with a link to it, to the channel's X account on a schedule.

Who can use it

Only its operator. VideoForge is not offered to the public, has no sign-up, and does not access anyone else's accounts or data.

How VideoForge handles account data is described in its privacy policy.
